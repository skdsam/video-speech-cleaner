# Speech Cleaner

## Tagline

Cleaner speech. Same timing. Completely private.

## Short product-card description

Automatically detect and mute filler sounds in video and audio without cutting the timeline or reducing video quality. Speech Cleaner runs locally on your Windows PC, keeping every recording private and perfectly in sync.

## Full product description

Speech Cleaner gives creators, editors, podcasters, and presenters a faster way to polish spoken content. Its local speech engine detects common hesitation sounds such as “um,” “uh,” “erm,” and “err,” then places precise mute regions directly on an interactive waveform.

Review every detection, preview the original audio, adjust boundaries, add custom mute regions, and choose exactly what should be applied before export. Instead of cutting sections from the recording, Speech Cleaner mutes them while preserving the original timeline, video stream, and audio/video synchronization.

Everything runs locally on your computer. There are no uploads, accounts, subscriptions, or cloud-processing delays.

## Feature highlights

- Private, offline speech analysis
- Automatic filler-sound detection
- Interactive waveform and detection review
- Preview, apply, edit, or remove individual mute regions
- Add custom mute regions manually
- Preserves the original timeline and synchronization
- Copies the original video stream without re-encoding it
- Lightweight installer with guided first-run component setup
